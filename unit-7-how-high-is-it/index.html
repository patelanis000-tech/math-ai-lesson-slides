From pattern to notation

Three ratios. Three names.

The reference angle decides which side is opposite and which is adjacent.

sine
sin θ = O / H
opposite ÷ hypotenuse
cosine
cos θ = A / H
adjacent ÷ hypotenuse
tangent
tan θ = O / A
opposite ÷ adjacent

Check

Calculator in degree mode. Draw the angle you are using.

For the height task

If you know horizontal distance and angle of elevation, tangent is usually the useful ratio.
