HL CONNECTION · COMPARE METHODS

Why the formulas agree

Let u = AB and v = AC be the two vectors starting at A. We will connect the dot product to the cosine rule for this same triangle.

Write the vector from B to C in terms of u and v. Try expanding its squared magnitude before revealing the steps.

BC = v − u

|v − u|² = (v − u) · (v − u)

= |u|² + |v|² − 2u · v

BC² = AB² + AC² − 2AB × AC cos A

u · v = |u||v| cos A

A → B → C and A → C have the same displacement. Read the explanation below as you reveal each step.

Select “Reveal next step” to compare your reasoning. The vectors describe the same three sides used in the cosine rule.
