Read the whole model
P(t)=L1+Ce−kt
LMaximum / limiting value the model approaches.
CStarting position relative to the limit; shifts when rapid growth occurs.
kHow rapidly the transition towards the limit happens.
Why this shape?

Exponential growth does not know when to stop.

If growth keeps depending only on how much is already present, the model keeps accelerating.
Real systems often have a limit: space, resources, customers, people, capacity.
exponential model
Verhulst's idea
Let L be the maximum value the system can approach.
dPdt=kP(1−PL)
You do not need to manipulate this yet. Read it as a sentence:
P
how much is already present
1 − P/L
the fraction of capacity still available
Think When P gets close to L, what happens to the second factor?
It approaches 0, so growth slows. Solving this differential equation gives the logistic family we just investigated.
