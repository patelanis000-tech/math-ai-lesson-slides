12:48–12:58 · transfer

Can you see the hidden right triangle?

A warehouse is 24 m long, 18 m wide and 8 m high. A cable runs from one bottom corner to the opposite top corner.

  1. Find the cable’s horizontal projection across the floor.
  2. Find the cable length.
  3. Find the angle between the cable and the floor.
  4. Explain why using 24 m as the adjacent side would be incorrect.
Check
Floor diagonal = 30 m. Cable length = √(30² + 8²) ≈ 31.0 m. Angle with the floor = tan⁻¹(8/30) ≈ 14.9°.
