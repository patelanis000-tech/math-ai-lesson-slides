HL · Definition

One radian: arc length equals radius.

The angle is 1 radian when the arc length is equal to the radius.

θ = sr   radians
An arc is three radius-lengths long.
What is the angle in radians?

Use the same length unit for s and r. Radian measure tells us how many radius-lengths fit along the arc.
