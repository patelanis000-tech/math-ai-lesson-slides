Area Surveyor

Briefing: shade for the courtyard

A triangular shade structure has side lengths 18 m and 24 m, with an included angle of 53°. The fabric supplier charges RM36 per m².

  1. Draw and label a triangle. Identify the two known sides and the included angle.
  2. Choose the correct rule. Explain why it is more useful than base × height here.
  3. Find the area of fabric required, then estimate the cost.
Your teach-back: “Use this rule when …”
Check: A = ½(18)(24) sin 53° ≈ 172.5 m². Cost ≈ RM6,210. The perpendicular height is unavailable, but two sides and their included angle are known.
Triangulation Specialist

Briefing: swimmer safety zone

Lifeguard points A and B are 120 m apart. A swimmer at C is observed at an angle of 58° from A and 47° from B. A rescue boat is stationed at B.

  1. Sketch the situation and find the third angle.
  2. How far is the swimmer from B?
  3. The rescue-zone radius is 100 m. Is the swimmer inside it?
Your teach-back: “Use this rule when …”
Check: ∠C = 75°. BC = (120 sin 58°) / sin 75° ≈ 105.3 m. The swimmer is outside the rescue zone. A known side and its opposite angle pair unlock the sine rule.
Route Designer

Briefing: zip-line connection

Two access paths from a starting point are 31 m and 26 m, with an included angle of 38°. A zip line will connect their endpoints.

  1. Sketch and label the direct zip-line distance.
  2. Choose the correct rule and calculate the distance.
  3. Cables are supplied only in whole metres. What minimum length should be ordered?
Your teach-back: “Use this rule when …”
Check: c² = 31² + 26² − 2(31)(26) cos 38°, so c ≈ 19.2 m. Order 20 m. Two sides and their included angle unlock the cosine rule when the missing side is needed.

Step 03 · Mixed team mission

Three decisions. Three tools.

Your mixed group should contain one expert from each field team. Before calculating, every question needs a written rule choice and reason.

Mission A

Festival food-truck zone

A triangular zone has sides 14 m and 22 m, with an included angle of 61°. Find its area.

First write: Rule: ______ because ______.

Interpretation: How much temporary flooring is required?
Area rule. A = ½(14)(22) sin 61° ≈ 134.7 m².
Mission B

Coastal distance check

In triangle ABC, A = 42°, B = 71°, and a = 85 m. Find b.

First write: Rule: ______ because ______.

Interpretation: Which side is opposite the 71° angle?
Sine rule. b = (85 sin 71°) / sin 42° ≈ 120.1 m.
Mission C

Roof-truss connection

Two roof beams are 17 m and 24 m, meeting at an angle of 68°. Find the third side of the truss.

First write: Rule: ______ because ______.

Interpretation: Explain why the cosine rule fits this triangle.
Cosine rule. c = √[17² + 24² − 2(17)(24) cos 68°] ≈ 23.6 m.
